Welcome to
Hotel Simran International

Nestled in the bustling heart of Raipur, Hotel Simran International has been a beacon of warm hospitality and refined comfort for over two decades. Conveniently located on Main Road Pandri — just steps from the city's commercial hub and minutes from the railway station — our hotel seamlessly blends traditional Chhattisgarhi warmth with contemporary elegance.

Whether you're a business traveller seeking seamless connectivity, a family on a memorable holiday, or a couple celebrating a special occasion, we promise an experience that feels like home — only better. Every detail, from our thoughtfully appointed rooms to our aromatic multi-cuisine restaurant, is designed to make your stay truly unforgettable.
